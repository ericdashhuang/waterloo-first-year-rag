TITLE: Co-op earnings

Co-op earnings
According to our work term requirements , you must receive some form of compensation for your co-op work terms in order to receive credit. This compensation varies widely depending on your employer, your program of study, your work term level or your overall work experience.
We record and publish average earnings information annually in the charts for students and employers to use as a reference. Please note that employers are not required to follow these rates of pay, but can consult them when determining your salary.
