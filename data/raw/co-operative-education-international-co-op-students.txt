Work permit important notice
As of April 1, 2026, students no longer need a co-op work permit for co-op work terms. Co-op students with a valid study permit, which includes conditions that say you’re allowed to work “on campus”, are eligible to complete co-op work terms. For more information, visit the IRCC website , or contact the Regulated Canadian Immigration Consultant team .
