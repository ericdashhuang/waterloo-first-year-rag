Employer Information Sessions
Network and connect with potential employers
Meet employers hiring University of Waterloo co-op students and graduates. Learn about company culture, hiring timelines, work terms and career pathways directly from recruiters and employees.
Employer Information Sessions help you:
Learn what companies look for when hiring
Explore industries and career paths
Build confidence before applying
Make meaningful connections with employers and alumni

Update your résumé and LinkedIn profile
Prepare some questions to ask the employer
During the session:
Dress appropriately to make a good first impression
Introduce yourself to the employer
Participate in any Q&A
Take note of key hiring details
After the session:
Follow-up with any connections through email or LinkedIn
Apply to roles that interest you
Reflect on what you learned and how it applies to your career journey
