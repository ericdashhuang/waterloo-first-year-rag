Parents and supporters
Supporting your student through co-op? We're here to help them every step of the way!
No other school does co-op like Waterloo. We have an entire department dedicated to helping students find meaningful work experiences that will prepare them for their future careers.
From professional development courses to specialized career counselling to regionally based on-the-job support, our team is here to help your student as they navigate the co-op process and all the decisions that come with it.
