CCD workshops and events
The Centre for Career Development (CCD) offers a wide range of workshops that are available to all students (co-op, regular, undergraduate, graduate and postdocs) to help you meet your career goals.
In these workshops, CCD Career Advisors cover popular career topics like:
Résumé writing and online profiles (e.g., LinkedIn)
Interview preparation and effectively showcasing your skills
Job search strategies and career pathway exploration
Negotiating job offers successfully
How to attend a workshop
Watch past recorded sessions
Explore our library of recorded workshops for quick, practical career support. Whether you’re making decisions about your future, searching for a job or preparing for life after graduation, you’ll find helpful guidance on popular topics like résumés, interviews and further education preparation.
