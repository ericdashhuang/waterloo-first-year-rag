Get career ready with CareerHub
CareerHub is your go-to space for career support at any time. Whether you’re just starting to explore your options or preparing for your next opportunity, CareerHub gives you 24/7 access to tools, resources and guidance to help you move forward with confidence. Identity and your career
Book an appointment with the Centre for Career Development
You can connect with the Centre for Career Development (CCD) team of Career Advisors at any stage of your journey. They’re here to support you as you explore options, build confidence and navigate your next steps.
Career Advisors can help you:
Explore what’s possible based on your strengths, interests and curiosity
Get ready for your job search, including applications, networking and interviews
Make the most of your experiences by setting goals and navigating uncertainty
Find the right supports for your unique career and work journey
Visit the CCD website to book an appointment, check drop-in hours, or explore workshops and events. You can schedule an in-person or virtual appointment through WaterlooWorks.
Get a free professional headshot!
CCD offers free professional headshots every term to help students build a strong personal brand and make a great first impression. Whether you're updating your LinkedIn profile, applying for jobs, or preparing for networking events, a professional headshot can help you stand out and present yourself confidently.
