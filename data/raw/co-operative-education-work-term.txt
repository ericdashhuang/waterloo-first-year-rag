Success at work
Looking to make the most of your work term? CareerHub offers practical tips and insights to help you succeed in the workplace, from understanding workplace culture and building strong relationships to navigating challenges and knowing your rights.
Stay connected with the co-op community!
Connecting with others can make your work term experience even more rewarding. Take advantage of opportunities to learn from your peers, share experiences and stay engaged with the co-op community. Whether you’re reading student stories on the blog or joining conversations in the Co-op Connection Discord, these spaces can help you feel supported, build connections and pick up helpful tips throughout your work term.
