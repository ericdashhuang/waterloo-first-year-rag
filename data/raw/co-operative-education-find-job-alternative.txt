TITLE: Alternative co-op opportunities

Alternative co-op opportunities
Not all co-op experiences look the same. Alternative co-op opportunities give you different ways to build skills, gain experience and explore your interests. Whether it’s developing real-world projects, working with startups or launching your own business, these options can be helpful if you’re looking for flexibility or want to try something outside of a traditional work term.
Waterloo Experience (WE) Accelerate program
Develop in-demand skills while working on real-world projects in a flexible, project-based environment. WE Accelerate is available to students in their first work term , making it a great option to build experience and confidence early in your co-op journey.
WE Accelerate program eligibility and benefits
Bridging Entrepreneurs to Students (BETS) program
Work with a local startup and gain hands-on experience in an entrepreneurial environment. BETS is designed for students in their first work term and offers a chance to contribute directly to growing businesses.
How to apply for the BETS program
Enterprise Co-op (E Co-op) program
Start your own business while earning co-op credit. E Co-op is available during any work term , giving you the flexibility to pursue your entrepreneurial ideas at any point in your co-op journey.
