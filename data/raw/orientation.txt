TITLE: Orientation

Orientation
First Year Orientation
First Year Orientation is a collaboration between the Waterloo Undergraduate Student Association (WUSA) , the Student Success Office (SSO) , and the University's six faculties.
International Orientation
International Orientation is a collaboration between the International Experience Centre (IEC) , Student Success Office (SSO) and the University's six faculties.
Graduate Orientation
Graduate Orientation is a collaboration between the Student Success Office (SSO) and the Graduate Student Association (GSA) .
Warrior Family Orientation
Warrior Family Orientation is a collaboration between the Student Success Office (SSO) and the University's six faculties.
